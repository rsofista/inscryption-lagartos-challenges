# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Fix: No First Turn Kill / No Phase Turn Kill froze the game (camera stuck on the attack) whenever they cut combat
  damage.
- The Trapper sells the regular pelts at their regular prices under the tribe/cost challenges (they used to all
  be Annoying Rabbit Pelts at the Rabbit Pelt price).
- New challenge: Forbidden Cards (-5). The Great Kraken joins the rare offers; the Royal's crew, Strange Frog, Pack
  Mule, The Smoke, Greater Smoke and Starvation join the card choices.
- New starter deck: Royal's Crew (Mole Seaman, Skeleton Crew, Zombie Parrot), with pixel portraits for the three.
- Shorter challenge descriptions (No First Turn Kill, No Phase Turn Kill, Forbidden Cards, Annoying Trades) so
  they fit on the challenge screen in every language.
- Starter deck icons now show up: they were opaque pictures, but the deck screen only uses an icon's shape. Each pelt deck
  shows its 3 pelts stacked (Rabbit plain, Wolf striped, Golden gridded); Royal's Crew shows a skull with an eye patch.

## 1.0.0

- Initial release: 25 new challenges, plus Unfair Hand, Worthy Squirrel, Annoying Trades and the six pelt starter decks
  from the Unfair Hand and Pelt Starter Hands mods (which this mod replaces), plus two new decks (Golden and Wolf Pelts,
  Golden Pelts). Every active challenge icon has a yellow frame.
